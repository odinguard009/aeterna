HOW TO PLAY

THE BASICS

A match in five minutes: goal, turn, cores, special cards and controls.

Goal

Reduce the opposing Life from 5 to 0 by attacking with your Spirits, or make the opponent run out of cards at the start of their turn.

A turn

  • Start / Core: you gain a core (the first player skips it on turn 1).
  • Draw: draw one card. Refresh: exhausted cards stand up and Trash cores return to the Reserve.
  • Main: summon Spirits, deploy Nexuses, cast Magic, set a Burst, move cores, use effects. Press NEXT to move on.
  • Attack: choose a Spirit to attack. The defender may block with one ready Spirit; the higher BP wins (ties destroy both). An unblocked attack removes Life equal to its symbols.
  • End: the turn passes. In Standard there is also a second Main step.

Cores and costs

  • Pay a card's cost with Reserve cores (or cores from your Spirits). Matching-color cards on your field reduce the cost.
  • A Spirit's level depends on the cores on it; more cores means more BP. A Spirit with too few cores is depleted and goes to the Trash.
  • The Soul Core is a special core; some effects send it to the Trash, a Nexus or the Void.

Special cards

  • Burst: set a card face down in the Burst area; it flips by itself when its condition happens.
  • Flash: Magic and effects usable during the two Flash windows of a battle (defender first).
  • Advent / Contract Advent / Manifest: stack or summon a stronger card using the Soul Core. Buttons appear on the card in your Hand.
  • Soul State / Void: extra zones shown in the top-right corner (Soul State) and the side panel (Void).

Controls

  • Tap a card to open its actions; tap a zone (Trash, Soul State, Burst…) to look inside it. Long-press a card to zoom it.
  • On a PC, hover a card to read it. Keyboard: Space or Enter = Next step, L = game log, Esc = close.
  • The game log (📜) lists everything that happened. Settings let you change the game speed and sounds.

CARD LIBRARY

LIBRARY

Card data, your card images and deck lists. Everything stays on this device.

1Card data (JSON)

Load your own card list. Only Spirit and Nexus cards work in this build. Imported cards have no coded effect, so their text is shown for reference only.

2Card images

Pick image files from your own device. A file is matched to a card by (1) the image field in your card data, so any file name works, (2) the card ID (for example BS76-001.png), or (3) the exact card name. Images stay in this browser only and are never uploaded.

3Deck list (text)

One card per line: 3 BS44-001 or 3x Card Name. Lines starting with # are ignored.
