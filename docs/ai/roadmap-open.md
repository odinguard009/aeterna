## F: Foundation first (owner, 2026-10-20 17:00: "infrastructure before card programming so new cards do not shake the system"; Opus)

Changing those after thousands of cards are authored means touching every card again, so they are fixed first. UI restyles (U5-U8), languages, server, database and card images do not change the card format and wait.
